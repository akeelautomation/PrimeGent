Flannel Shirt Outfits for Men: Casual Style Made Easy

June 7, 20266 min read

Discover how to style a men's flannel shirt for effortless casual looks, from weekend adventures to relaxed evenings.

Quick win

Layer an open flannel shirt over a plain, solid-colored t-shirt in a neutral or complementary shade for an instantly relaxed and stylish look.

Start here

The flannel shirt is a cornerstone of casual menswear, offering warmth, texture, and a rugged aesthetic that’s both timeless and versatile. Its inherent comfort and relaxed vibe make it a go-to for countless occasions, from weekend errands to outdoor excursions. When styled correctly, a flannel shirt can elevate a simple outfit into something with character and depth.

Building a great outfit around a flannel shirt doesn't require a complex wardrobe. The image showcases a classic combination: a plaid flannel worn open over a plain t-shirt, paired with relaxed-fit denim and high-top sneakers. This ensemble embodies a practical, off-duty style that’s easy to replicate. We’ll explore the nuances of this look, from selecting the right flannel and base layer to choosing complementary pants and footwear.

1. Choosing the Right Flannel Shirt

The visual appeal of a flannel shirt often starts with its pattern and color. The green and brown plaid in the image offers a classic, earthy tone that pairs well with denim and other casual fabrics. When selecting your own flannel, consider the scale of the plaid – smaller checks can appear more subtle, while larger patterns make a bolder statement.

Fit is paramount when it comes to flannel shirts. The shirt in the image appears to have a relaxed fit, allowing it to be comfortably worn open over another layer. This is ideal for a casual, unbuttoned style. If you prefer a more streamlined look, opt for a slimmer fit, but ensure it's not so tight that it restricts movement or looks uncomfortable when layered.

2. The Perfect Base Layer: T-Shirts

Beneath the flannel, a well-chosen t-shirt provides a foundation for the outfit. The navy crewneck t-shirt in the image is a solid choice because it's a neutral color that doesn't compete with the plaid. A plain, solid-colored tee is essential for this layering technique. Think about basic colors like white, grey, black, or navy, which act as versatile canvases.

The contrast between the t-shirt and the flannel is key to making the outfit visually interesting. A dark t-shirt under a lighter or more colorful flannel creates a grounded look, while a lighter t-shirt can make the flannel pop. Consider the texture of the t-shirt as well. A smooth cotton tee offers a clean base, but a subtly textured knit can add another layer of visual interest without being distracting.

3. Denim: The Classic Pairing

Jeans are the quintessential partner for a flannel shirt, and the light wash, relaxed-fit denim in the image perfectly embodies this classic combination. The relaxed fit offers comfort and a slightly retro feel, aligning with the casual nature of the flannel. When choosing jeans to pair with flannel, consider the wash and fit. Darker washes tend to look slightly more polished, making them suitable for smart-casual settings, while lighter washes lean more towards.

The way the jeans are styled also contributes to the overall aesthetic. The cuffed hem on the jeans in the image is a deliberate detail that adds a touch of intentionality to the casual look. It helps to showcase the footwear and prevents the denim from bunching up around the ankles, especially with high-top sneakers. When cuffing your jeans, aim for a consistent width – about one to two inches is usually sufficient.

4. Footwear for a Casual Vibe

The choice of footwear significantly influences the overall formality and style of a flannel shirt outfit. The black high-top Converse-style sneakers in the image are an excellent match for the relaxed, slightly vintage feel of the ensemble. High-tops add a bit of edge and visual interest, complementing the casual nature of the jeans and flannel. They work particularly well with cuffed jeans, as seen in the image, drawing attention to the footwear.

Beyond high-top sneakers, other footwear options can adapt the flannel outfit for different casual settings. Clean, low-top sneakers offer a more understated look, while sturdy work boots or chukka boots can add a rugged, outdoorsy feel, especially with darker wash jeans or corduroy pants. For a slightly more refined casual look, consider loafers or desert boots, provided the rest of the outfit maintains a relaxed aesthetic.

5. Accessories to Complete the Look

Accessories can transform a basic flannel outfit into a more personalized and complete look. The image includes a simple silver chain necklace with a circular pendant, adding a subtle metallic accent without being ostentatious. This type of understated jewelry works well with casual wear, providing a touch of detail. A leather or woven bracelet, as seen in the image, further enhances the rugged, masculine feel of the outfit.

The messenger bag shown in the image is another practical accessory that enhances the outfit's utility and style. Its durable material and classic design complement the rugged aesthetic of the flannel and denim. A well-chosen bag can add both functionality and a visual anchor to your outfit. Consider the size and style of the bag based on your needs – a crossbody bag, a backpack, or a tote can all work depending on.

6. Building Your Flannel Outfit Formula

The core outfit formula demonstrated here is simple yet effective: a plaid flannel shirt (worn open or closed), a solid-colored t-shirt, relaxed-fit jeans, and casual sneakers. This formula provides a reliable template for numerous casual occasions. To adapt it, consider variations in flannel patterns and colors, different washes and fits of denim, and a range of complementary footwear.

Beyond the basic formula, think about layering for different weather conditions. In cooler weather, a heavier flannel can be worn over a long-sleeve t-shirt or a thin knit, and then layered under a heavier jacket like a denim or bomber jacket. For warmer days, a lighter flannel can be worn on its own or over a simple tee, with the sleeves rolled up.

Checklist

  • Choose a flannel shirt with a plaid pattern and colors that complement your existing wardrobe.
  • Ensure your flannel shirt has a relaxed fit for comfortable layering, or a slim fit for a cleaner silhouette.
  • Pair your flannel with a plain, solid-colored t-shirt in a neutral or complementary shade.
  • Opt for relaxed-fit or straight-leg jeans in a wash that suits the desired level of casualness.
  • Select casual footwear like high-top sneakers, low-top sneakers, or work boots that match the outfit's vibe.
  • Add subtle accessories like a simple necklace, bracelet, or a functional messenger bag to personalize your look.
